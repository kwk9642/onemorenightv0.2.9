再活一晚 One More Night — Suno 音效／配樂清單
貼進 Suno 的英文 prompt。檔名是遊戲要用的 id。

01. title.mp3（已有）
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. A looping title theme for a text-based zombie shelter sim. Dusty American Midwest dusk, boarded farmhouse, analog radio hiss under a slow guitar and low cello. Melancholy, not epic, not heroic. Sparse piano after 30 seconds. Feels like one more night might be the last. 90-110 BPM, minor key, loopable 2 minutes.

02. hub.mp3（已有）
Instrumental only, no vocals. Interior shelter ambience as music: wood creak, distant generator, muted folk guitar, analog tape warmth. Quiet colony management, Kentucky autumn, oil lamp, not a battle theme. Slow 70-85 BPM, looping, room tone mixed with music. Safe but never comfortable.

03. mission.mp3（已有）
Instrumental only, no vocals. Stealth scavenger loop for walking a looted American town. Tension without drums at first, then a dry heartbeat kick. Empty mall air, fluorescent buzz as texture, detuned guitar, industrial pad. Not action Hollywood. Paranoia. 95 BPM, loopable.

04. combat.mp3（已有）
Instrumental only, no vocals. Close-quarters zombie fight layer. Fast dry percussion, distorted bass, metallic hits, no orchestra swell, no choir. Ugly, physical, short loop 45-70 seconds so it can duck under the mission track. Relentless, not heroic.

05. occupy.mp3（已有）
Instrumental only, no vocals. A held-breath theme for a stranger at the boarded door: stay, leave, or become an enemy. Slow pulse, radio voice almost forming words but never lyrics. Moral weight. 60 BPM, sparse piano and bowed bass.

06. end.mp3（已有）
Instrumental only, no vocals. Permadeath end: the last survivor is gone. Not a movie credits fanfare. Empty house, wind in plywood, one guitar phrase that does not resolve. Cold. 2 minutes, fade on tape hiss.

07. hub-night.mp3
Instrumental only, no vocals. Same shelter as hub but after dark: everyone is asleep or afraid. Lower, slower, more wind against boards, distant dogs, one analog synth like a dying fridge. Loop for the overnight wait. 55-65 BPM.

08. hub-hope.mp3
Instrumental only, no vocals. Rare good morning in the wasteland: food for a week, nobody bit. Warm analog keys, soft acoustic guitar, still dusty, not cheerful pop. A crack of light through nailed plywood. Loopable, 80 BPM.

09. hub-starving.mp3
Instrumental only, no vocals. The cupboards are empty. Stomach-cramp drone, dry mouth percussion of clock ticks, no melody for a long time, then a thin harmonica far away. Anxiety loop for a hungry colony. No vocals.

10. hub-sick.mp3
Instrumental only, no vocals. Someone in the house is bitten or fevered. Medical horror without jumpscares: slow dripping, detuned piano, analog tape wobble, heartbeat very quiet. Loop. Do not use choir.

11. mission-house.mp3

12. mission-mall.mp3
